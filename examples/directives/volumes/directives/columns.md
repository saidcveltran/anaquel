# Columns

Side-by-side blocks that stack on a narrow screen. A `column` may have a `width` from 1 to 6
that says how much of the free room it takes compared with the others.

`````markdown
::::columns
:::column width=2
The wide column.
:::
:::column
The narrow one.
:::
::::
`````

::::columns
:::column width=2
The wide column holds the main idea, written at a length that shows how it wraps when there
is little room.
:::
:::column
The narrow one.
:::
::::
